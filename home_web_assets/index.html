Welcome to Linguar Hub

Before the Trello-based panels (Audit, Hygiene, Snapshot, APA…) can load, you need to connect your account and confirm your folders.

Open ⚙ Settings (bottom of the sidebar) and set:

  • Trello API key and Trello token — your Trello login for the boards.
  • Folder paths — these are auto-detected on first launch; just confirm they point to the right places.

Your settings are saved to your own PC (%APPDATA%\Linguar Hub) — nothing is shared.
