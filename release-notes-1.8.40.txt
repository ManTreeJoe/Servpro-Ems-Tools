OneLoss 1.8.40 — Unified file imports

Import files is now the single entry point for local files, CompanyCam photos and Trello attachments.
CompanyCam and Trello galleries support grouped selection, image previews and destination review before importing.
CompanyCam quick import avoids waiting for every photo's tags. Optional tag-based organization remains available.
Completed CompanyCam imports no longer add a status-screen step when reopening the gallery.
Adds right-click text editing support, a smaller comments header and compact job-link controls.
Adds notification sound choices.
Retains the 1.8.38 Job Log and 1.8.39 comment-posting fixes.

Known limitation: older Trello downloads without attachment IDs in their filenames may not be recognized as duplicates.
Automated checks use controlled fixtures; they do not post comments or import customer files.
